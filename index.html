Patient reply ratings

Which reply would a real patient give?

You will read one psychiatric interview with a simulated patient, a turn at a time. At each turn the patient has three possible replies. Mark the most realistic and the least realistic.

The case

How to rate

  • Read the conversation so far, then the doctor's latest line.
  • Ask: which reply sounds most like how a real patient with this history would answer at this point?
  • Mark one reply Best and one Worst.
  • If all three would be equally realistic, choose All of the above. If none would be, choose None of these.
  • A comment is optional. Use it when something is clinically wrong.
  • Your answers save on this device as you go. When you finish a part, download your answers and send the file to the research team.
